Dubai has positioned itself as the brand capital of the Middle East and North Africa. The city's extraordinary growth, its concentration of global headquarters, and its aspiration to become the world's most business-friendly city have created a brand design market that is both sophisticated and intensely competitive.

Global agency networks have established significant Dubai presences. Regional independents have emerged with distinctive approaches. And a new generation of digital-first brand studios now serves Dubai's FinTech, PropTech, and startup communities. Here are the top 10 branding agencies in Dubai for 2026.
