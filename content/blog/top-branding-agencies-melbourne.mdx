Melbourne has always been the city where Australian design culture sets the standard. Its creative industry — cafés, fashion, architecture, film — has produced a brand sensibility that is distinctive from Sydney's more corporate aesthetic: warmer, more culturally grounded, and often more willing to take creative risk. Combined with a growing technology sector and Australia's second-largest financial services market, Melbourne's branding agencies serve a remarkably diverse client base. Here are the top 10 branding agencies in Melbourne for 2026.
