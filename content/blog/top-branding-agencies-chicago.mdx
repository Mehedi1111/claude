Chicago has one of the longest traditions in American advertising and brand design. The city was home to Leo Burnett — the man who created the Marlboro Man, Tony the Tiger, and the Jolly Green Giant — and that tradition of powerful, idea-led brand thinking still runs through the city's agency community today.

From major advertising networks to independent brand studios and digital-first boutiques, Chicago offers businesses a wide range of branding options. Here are the top 10 branding agencies in Chicago for 2026.
