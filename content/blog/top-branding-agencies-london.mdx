London sits alongside New York as one of the two most important cities in global brand design. The city produced some of the most influential brand identities of the 20th century and continues to set design standards that agencies worldwide follow. Its branding market spans world-class global consultancies, razor-sharp boutique studios, and some of the best independent design talent anywhere.

For UK founders and businesses, the challenge is identifying which London branding agency matches your stage, budget, and brief. This list does that work for you — ranking the top 10 branding agencies in London for 2026.

## 2. Wolff Olins (London)

**Specialisation:** Brand transformation, brand strategy, digital brand systems

Wolff Olins was founded in London in 1965 and remains one of the most influential brand consultancies globally. The London office has produced landmark identities for BT, Orange, Tate Modern, and Transport for London. The firm specialises in brand transformation for large organisations — repositioning, renaming, and rebuilding brand systems at scale. Best suited for enterprise clients and major public sector organisations.

## 3. Pentagram (London)

**Specialisation:** Multi-discipline design — identity, editorial, wayfinding, environmental

Pentagram's London office is the founding studio of the partnership, established in 1972. It operates with the same independent principal structure as the New York office — each principal leads their own team and client relationships within the larger firm. Work spans corporate identity, arts and culture, wayfinding, and publication design. One of the most creatively accomplished studios in the UK.

**Specialisation:** Graphic design, brand identity, visual communications

London offers every level of branding service from every price point. Matching studio to need:
