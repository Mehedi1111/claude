Montreal occupies a unique space in North America's creative landscape.

Canada's second-largest city brings together French creative tradition, English business pragmatism, and a genuine counterculture — producing a design scene that is sophisticated, internationally connected, and unlike anywhere else on the continent. The city's video game industry (Ubisoft, EA, Warner Bros. Games), thriving AI research cluster, and extraordinary cultural output (Cirque du Soleil, the Montreal International Jazz Festival) create a creative economy that generates some of North America's best digital agencies.
