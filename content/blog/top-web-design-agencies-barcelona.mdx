Barcelona is an unusual creative market.

The city has a deep design heritage — rooted in the Catalan modernisme movement, the influence of Gaudí's organic visual language, and a design culture that values distinctiveness and craft over convention.

At the same time, Barcelona has become one of Europe's most active startup and technology ecosystems — hosting Mobile World Congress, a major hub for tech companies, and a fast-growing community of digital businesses.

The result: an agency market that combines European creative ambition with a more accessible cost structure than London, Amsterdam, or Paris.

Here are the top 10 web design agencies in Barcelona for 2027.
