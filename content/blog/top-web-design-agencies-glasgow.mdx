Glasgow is a city that confounds expectations.

Scotland's largest city — once synonymous with heavy industry and the Clyde shipyards — has reinvented itself as one of the UK's most vibrant creative cities. The city that gave the world the Glasgow School of Art, Charles Rennie Mackintosh, and a music scene of remarkable depth has a creative culture that is proudly distinctive. Glasgow's design agencies carry this character: independent, bold, and unconcerned with London's approval.
