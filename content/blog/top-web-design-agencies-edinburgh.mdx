Edinburgh is an underrated web design market.

The city has Scotland's most concentrated professional services sector, a thriving tech scene centred around the Quartermile and Leith developments, world-class universities generating startup activity, and a tourism and hospitality industry that demands premium digital work.

What Edinburgh hasn't always had is agency talent to match London quality at London prices. That's changing — both through a maturing local market and through the ability of Scottish businesses to work with excellent remote agencies without paying for London square footage.

Here are the top 10 web design agencies in Edinburgh for 2027.
