New York City is the most competitive branding market in the world. The city is home to legendary global firms that have shaped the visual identities of Fortune 500 companies, boutique studios serving emerging startups, and everything in between. Choosing the right branding partner in this market is not about finding the most famous name — it is about finding the studio whose specialisation, process, and price point match what your business actually needs.

This list ranks the top 10 branding agencies serving New York businesses in 2026 — starting with the studio we believe delivers the best combination of quality, specialisation, and value for founders and growing brands.

## How to Choose the Right New York Branding Agency

The agencies on this list range from $50 (Evoke's AI vectorization) to $500,000+ (enterprise brand transformations at firms like Pentagram or Wolff Olins). Choosing correctly means matching budget, scope, and specialisation:

- **Startup or small business:** Evoke Studio — precise, affordable, fast
- **Growth-stage company needing a complete brand system:** Evoke Studio or Collins
- **Enterprise company with complex brand architecture:** Landor & Fitch, Siegel+Gale, or Lippincott
- **Global brand transformation with strategic consulting:** Wolff Olins or Interbrand
- **Entertainment, media, or technology brand:** Collins or Gretel
