Global agency networks maintain significant Singapore presences. Regional specialists have emerged with deep ASEAN market knowledge. And a growing FinTech and technology sector has created demand for precisely the kind of brand work that globally-minded studios like Evoke deliver. Here are the top 10 branding agencies in Singapore for 2026.
