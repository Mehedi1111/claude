A premium commercial seed and seedling supplier serving growers across the Southern United States needed a **seed and plant company logo design** that communicated professional-grade quality — not a hobbyist brand, not a generic agriculture mark, but a visual identity that would hold its own in trade settings, on packaging, and in supplier catalogues.

The brief was specific: incorporate a seed, a seedling (or part of a plant), and possibly a plant tray or icon. The challenge was combining those three elements into a single, clean logomark that didn't look like a clipart composition.

## What does this logo design include?

The final mark is a custom botanical logomark — geometric enough to be precise and scalable, organic enough to communicate growth and the natural product. It incorporates the required elements (seed form, seedling growth) without relying on literal illustration or clipart.

The delivered brand identity system includes:
- Custom botanical logomark in horizontal and icon-only configurations
- Colour palette with Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and hex specifications
- Typography system for all brand materials
- Usage guidelines covering minimum sizes, clear space, and background use

## How does Evoke Studio approach agricultural logo design?

Agricultural and botanical logos fail in one of two ways: they lean too heavily on literal clipart (a generic leaf, a stock sprout icon), or they strip out all organic reference in favour of generic corporate geometry. The approach here balances both requirements — a genuinely custom botanical mark that is:

- **Precise** — built on a mathematical grid, with every curve and proportion drawn from scratch in Adobe Illustrator
- **Scalable** — works at favicon size, on a seed packet, and on a trade show banner
- **Distinctive** — not a stock icon, not a template modification, but an original mark

![Seed company brand identity system — logo applications and brand guidelines by Evoke Studio](/portfolio/seed-plant-logo/image2.avif)

## Why does brand identity matter for agricultural suppliers?

Commercial growers and buyers in the Southern US evaluate suppliers on quality signals — and the brand is the first signal before the product arrives. A professional logo and brand identity communicates:

- **Credibility** at trade shows, in supplier catalogues, and on packaging
- **Consistency** across all buyer touchpoints — website, signage, delivery labels
- **Premium positioning** that justifies price points against cheaper commodity suppliers

For a business supplying professional-grade seeds and seedlings to commercial growers across Texas, Florida, Georgia, and the Carolinas, looking professional is not optional — it is the entry requirement.
