Parallel Labs is a deep-tech research organisation working at the intersection of quantum computing and machine learning. When they came to Evoke, they had a specific problem: every logo option they'd been shown looked like a SaaS productivity tool.

Their work is mathematically complex, physically grounded, and operating at the frontier of computational science. Their brand needed to reflect that without resorting to the visual language of generic technology companies.

<Callout type="note" title="Brief Excerpt">
"We want something that looks like it was designed by someone who has actually done mathematics. Not a friendly startup. Not enterprise software. Something that belongs in a research context."
</Callout>

## The Discovery Process

We began with a two-hour briefing session focused on understanding the science, not just the aesthetics.

Key brand attributes that emerged:

- **Precision** — Every measurement matters in quantum computing
- **Parallelism** — The core concept of their research architecture
- **Rigour** — Mathematical, not decorative
- **Forward direction** — Research-oriented, future-facing

We reviewed visual references from scientific institutions, mathematical publications, and precision instrument manufacturers — deliberately avoiding the technology sector's existing visual vocabulary.

## Concept Development

Three distinct directions were developed:

**Direction 1** explored a circular mark based on orbital patterns from quantum mechanics — elegant but too abstract for the brand positioning.

**Direction 2** used typographic construction — a custom letterform P built from mathematical construction lines. Clean and functional, but lacked the conceptual depth the brief called for.

**Direction 3** — the selected direction — derived from two offset parallelogram forms. When overlapped, the forms create a natural negative-space P in the intersection. The parallelogram itself references the mathematical object (a quadrilateral with parallel opposite sides) and visually communicates the concept of parallelism.

## Mathematical Construction

<Callout type="tip" title="The Grid System">
Every element of the mark is derived from a 12-unit grid. The parallelogram proportions are calculated from the golden ratio (1:1.618). The offset distance between the two forms equals exactly one grid unit. Nothing is placed by eye.
</Callout>

The construction:

1. Establish a 12-unit square grid
2. Define parallelogram proportions: width = 7 units, height = 4 units, skew angle = 22.5°
3. First form: positioned at origin
4. Second form: offset by 1 unit right, 1 unit down, rotated 180°
5. Intersection creates natural P negative space at exact grid centre

This level of mathematical precision means the mark can be reproduced from specifications alone — no creative interpretation required.
