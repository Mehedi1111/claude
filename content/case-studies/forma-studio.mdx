Typography is the most technically demanding element of any logo reconstruction. AI generators frequently distort letterforms, merge characters at connection points, and produce spacing that appears acceptable at small sizes but reveals serious problems at display scale.

Forma Studio — a premium interior design practice — brought us a wordmark that had become central to their identity. Generated in Midjourney, the letterforms had a distinctive architectural quality that the founding partners loved.

The problem emerged when they tried to use the mark on their studio entrance signage.

## The Typography Issues

At 600mm wide — the size required for their studio entrance — the issues were immediately apparent:

- The 'F' and 'O' characters had merged at a connection point, creating an artifact that looked like a ligature but wasn't intentional
- Stroke weights varied between characters by up to 15% — invisible at small sizes, obvious at large
- The spacing between 'R' and 'M' was almost double the spacing between other character pairs
- The baseline was not horizontally consistent — it drifted approximately 2px across the length of the wordmark

Each of these problems requires a different technical solution.

## Typeface Analysis

The wordmark appeared to reference a commercial display typeface. We used a combination of automated typeface recognition tools and manual comparison against major type libraries to identify the likely source family.

<Callout type="tip" title="The Identification Process">
AI generators often produce letterforms that are heavily influenced by a specific typeface but modified enough that they don't exactly match. We identify the source family to establish the correct proportional reference, then reconstruct the letterforms with appropriate modifications that make the final mark distinctly owned by the client.
</Callout>

After analysis, we identified the underlying typeface family and the specific weight and optical size that matched most closely with the AI-generated intent. This allowed us to establish correct proportional references for each character.

## Reconstruction

Each character was rebuilt as a clean vector path:

| Character | Issue | Resolution |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| F | Merged with O artifact | Rebuilt as separate clean path |
| O | Connection artifact | Closed path, correct optical size |
| R | Inconsistent weight | Weight standardised to median |
| M | Spacing error | Kerning pair corrected |
| A | Stroke inconsistency | Terminals adjusted to match F, O |

The baseline drift was corrected by establishing a mathematical baseline and aligning all character origins to it. The result is a wordmark where every character is optically balanced to its neighbours.
