Nexus is building infrastructure tooling for AI application deployment. Their platform helps engineering teams move models from research to production faster and more reliably. By early 2025, they had strong ARR, a growing customer base, and a Series A roadshow beginning in four weeks.

Their brand was not ready for the roadshow.

## The Starting Point

The founding team's CTO had generated the Nexus mark in Midjourney: a precise octagonal geometric form with internal structural detail that communicated system architecture, layers, and technical precision. The concept was strong. The file was a 2048×2048 PNG.

No vector source. No CMYK values. No type documentation. Every version of the logo the team was using had been generated from that same PNG, resulting in multiple slightly different versions across their pitch deck, website, and LinkedIn page.

<Callout type="warning" title="The Investor Deck Problem">
When the same logo appears differently on the cover page, the team slide, and the footer — different shades, slightly different proportions, different crops — it signals to sophisticated investors that the company operates without rigorous standards. In a deck where every slide is a proxy for operational discipline, brand inconsistency is a liability.
</Callout>

## The Reconstruction

The octagonal form presented an interesting geometric challenge: the internal structural detail suggested a specific mathematical proportion system, but the Midjourney output had introduced subtle inconsistencies in the symmetry that needed to be corrected in the reconstruction rather than preserved.

We identified the underlying grid — an 8-point rotational symmetry on a 100-unit circle — and rebuilt the mark to precise geometric specifications. The internal detail was reconstructed from the same proportional system. The result was a mark that was visually identical to the original but mathematically correct.

**Reconstruction specifications:**
- 8-fold rotational symmetry, 45° intervals
- Internal elements at 0.618× the outer radius (golden ratio)
- Stroke weights derived from grid proportions, not the raster output
- Corner radii unified across all 8 vertices

## The Brand System

Beyond the mark, we built a complete Series A brand kit:

| Deliverable | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Master vector | AI + SVG, documented grid |
| Colour system | 4 colours, all print + digital values |
| Typography | Inter + Space Mono pairing spec |
