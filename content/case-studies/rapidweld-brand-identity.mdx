**RapidWeld brand identity** — a welding and industrial fabrication company that needed a visual identity strong enough to hold its own in the industrial sector, distinctive enough to stand apart from the generic trade company aesthetic, and versatile enough to perform from workwear embroidery to full-side van livery.

The welding and fabrication sector is dominated by visual clichés: spark effects, flame imagery, bold sans-serifs with no mark, generic shield or gear motifs. The RapidWeld brief called for something with genuine visual intelligence — a brand that communicated industrial strength and precision without reaching for the obvious.

## The R+W lettermark: speed and precision in a single mark

The RapidWeld identity is built around a custom geometric lettermark:

- **R+W construction** — the brand's initials combined into a single unified mark, not just placed side by side
- **Integrated weld motif** — a graphic element referencing weld-point precision, built into the geometric form rather than applied as decoration
- **Mathematical grid** — every proportion derived from a grid system, ensuring the mark reads as precise and intentional at any scale

The lettermark functions as a standalone icon and as the primary mark — giving the brand maximum flexibility across all industrial applications.

## Colour system: yellow-green and black for industrial authority

The palette is built for maximum visual impact in industrial deployment:

- **Yellow-green (#e2f50a)** — energetic, modern, and immediately distinctive in industrial contexts where colour signals authority and visibility
- **Black** — communicates precision, seriousness, and technical credibility
- **High contrast** — the combination ensures legibility at vehicle livery scale, across large-format signage, and at small embroidery sizes simultaneously

This is not an office brand palette. It is a palette designed to perform on a van travelling at 70mph, on a worksite banner in direct sunlight, and on a chest logo stitched at 25mm.

![RapidWeld colour system and brand identity applications by Evoke Studio](/portfolio/rapidweld-logo/image2.avif)

## Industrial brand identity built for real-world deployment

Industrial and trade company identities fail when they are designed for presentations and not for production. The RapidWeld identity was stress-tested against every application from the first concept:

- **Vehicle livery** — mark reads at speed, at distance, in full sun and low light
- **Workwear** — lettermark holds at embroidery scale (25mm) with no detail loss
- **Site signage** — high contrast palette ensures legibility at distance
- **Digital** — mark scales from 16px favicon to full-bleed website hero

For a welding and fabrication company operating in competitive industrial markets across the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia, a professional brand identity is not a cosmetic choice — it is a direct signal of operational quality to commercial clients.
