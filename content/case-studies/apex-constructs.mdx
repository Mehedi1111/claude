Dan Reeves had been a project manager at a regional construction firm for twelve years before he started Apex Constructs. The brand — a Midjourney-generated geometric mark with the company initials and construction theme — was the first thing he'd designed for himself. He needed it to look right from day one.

The first project was due to start in four weeks. The vehicles needed to be wrapped. The site hoarding needed to go up. Three different suppliers had all said the same thing: the file couldn't be used.

## Three Rejections in One Week

**Vehicle wrap supplier 1** (vinyl wrap, 8 vans and trucks): *"File is a JPEG. We need vector artwork. Cannot guarantee wrap quality or alignment from a raster file."*

**Vehicle wrap supplier 2** (cross-checked with the same file): *"We can work with vector, not raster. The PNG you've sent won't hold quality on a vehicle at full-scale print."*

**Sign company** (4m × 1.5m hoardings, CNC-cut lettering): *"Our CNC router works from vector path data. Your file has no path data — it's an image. We can't cut from this."*

Dan had four weeks. The project start date was fixed by contract. He needed all three suppliers to accept files and begin production simultaneously.

<Callout type="note" title="Why Construction Branding Can't Use Raster Files">
Vehicle wraps are printed at sizes where a 2048px image has an effective resolution of under 20 DPI. Site hoardings at 4m width need artwork at equivalent resolution to produce sharp results. CNC routers work from mathematical path coordinates, not pixel grids. All three production processes require vector files — there is no workaround.
</Callout>

## The Reconstruction

We rebuilt the Apex mark from scratch in under 24 hours, using the Midjourney PNG purely as a visual reference.

The mark's geometric form — angular A-shapes forming a structural apex, with the company wordmark beneath — translated well to vector. The key technical decisions:

**For vehicle wraps:** The mark was delivered in 2-colour only (process black and safety orange) with no gradients. Vehicle wrap vinyl production works best with spot colours — gradients can produce banding visible on large format output. A gradient-free version with clean spot colour separations is always more reliable.

**For hoardings:** The file was structured at 1:10 scale (40cm × 15cm) representing the 400cm × 150cm final output. This is the workflow the CNC sign company used. The letterforms in the wordmark were outlined separately to allow independent cutting.

**For master archive:** A full-colour 4-colour version was delivered as the client's master file for future use cases.
