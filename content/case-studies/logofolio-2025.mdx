## The Work

Each mark in this collection was designed to solve a specific brand problem. The range of solutions reflects the range of problems:

**Technology companies** need marks that communicate precision and innovation — geometric forms built on grid systems, technical colour palettes, and wordmarks that signal engineering rigour.

**Professional services firms** need authority and trust — typographic wordmarks, restrained palettes, and identity systems that hold up under the scrutiny of high-value client relationships.

**Consumer brands** need emotional connection — organic forms, warm palettes, and brand systems that perform in product photography and social media contexts.

**Creative studios** need distinctive personality — marks with genuine character, systems with room for expression, and visual identities that demonstrate taste.

## The Design Principles

Every project in this collection was built on the same set of design principles:

**Grid construction** — every mark begins with a mathematical grid. Proportions are derived, not guessed. This ensures the mark holds its proportions across every scale and application.

**Optical correction** — geometric perfection is not visual perfection. Every mark receives optical corrections: adjusted stroke weights at critical junctions, balanced negative space, and terminal treatments that look right, not just mathematically correct.

**File integrity** — every mark is delivered with a clean vector structure: minimal anchor points, outlined strokes, documented colour values. The files work for every vendor, every time.

**System thinking** — a logo is not a brand. Every project in this collection was delivered with at least a colour system, typography specification, and usage guidelines — the minimum for a brand to operate consistently.
