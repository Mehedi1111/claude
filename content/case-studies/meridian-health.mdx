In healthcare, brand trust is built slowly. Meridian Health had invested significant time developing their visual identity — a clean, geometric AI-generated mark that tested well with their target audience of independent practitioners and health system administrators.

When their signage vendor rejected their logo file for the second time, they came to us.

## The Rejection Reports

Two vendor rejection reports told the story clearly:

**Rejection 1** (Large format printer): *"File contains open paths. Cannot guarantee print quality. Please supply corrected vector file."*

**Rejection 2** (Offset print, stationery supplier): *"Colour values do not match specified brand blue. Test print shows significant hue shift toward purple. Please supply Pantone-referenced file."*

Both rejections pointed to fixable technical problems. The logo itself was fine — the file was not.

## The Audit

We opened the Illustrator file that Meridian's designer had exported from a traced PNG and ran a full technical audit.

<Callout type="warning" title="What We Found">
11 open paths across the mark's components. 3 stroke elements not converted to outlines (a common source of vendor rejection — strokes render differently depending on the vendor's RIP settings). Colour specified as R:0 G:71 B:187 — a rich blue that shifts to approximately R:60 G:0 B:200 under CMYK conversion without colour management.
</Callout>

The specific issues:

| Issue | Count | Impact |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Open paths | 11 | File rejection, print artifacts |
| Unconverted strokes | 3 | Unpredictable weight at different scales |
| Incorrect CMYK conversion | 1 | Colour shift to purple |
| Missing bleed (stationery) | — | Edge trim issues |
| Redundant anchor points | ~400 | Fragile at scale |

## The Cleanup Process

Unlike full vectorisation, cleanup work preserves the existing structure where possible and corrects only the technical failures.

**Closing open paths**: Each open path was traced to its disconnected endpoint and closed with a mathematically consistent curve that matched the visual intent of the surrounding geometry.

**Stroke conversion**: The three unconverted strokes were expanded to outlined paths with the correct visual weight at the intended use size. Each was documented with its specification in the master file.

**Colour correction**: We established the correct CMYK equivalent for the brand blue through test printing on both coated and uncoated stock, then comparing results against the screen reference. The final specification:

- Pantone: 2728 C (coated) / 2728 U (uncoated)
- CMYK: C85 M50 Y0 K0
- RGB: R27 G108 B219
- Hex: #1B6CDB
