**EntroEdge investment brand identity** needed to communicate two things simultaneously: the precision and authority that institutional finance requires, and the forward-looking energy of a modern venture firm operating in the innovation economy. Most investment firm identities resolve this by defaulting to conservative — dark blue, serif typeface, understated mark. EntroEdge required more.

The brief called for a distinct and meaningful brand identity that communicated trust and innovation in equal measure — for a firm operating at the intersection of investment excellence and emerging market opportunity.

## What does the EntroEdge brand identity include?

The identity is built around a custom geometric lettermark:

- **The 'E' mark** — a custom letterform with an integrated upward arrow element, communicating growth and forward momentum within a single geometric construction
- **Colour system** — Navy Blue (trust, stability), Lavender (modern innovation), Pale Grey (supporting neutral) — specified across all colour systems
- **Typography** — a typographic system calibrated for precision and readability across pitch decks, digital platforms, and correspondence
- **Brand guidelines** — full usage rules for all applications

![EntroEdge investment firm brand identity — custom E lettermark with upward arrow by Evoke Studio](/portfolio/entroedge-logo/cover.avif)

## Why did EntroEdge choose a lettermark?

A lettermark — a logo built from the brand's initials — works particularly well for investment and professional services firms because:

1. **It scales to any context** — from a favicon on a web platform to a header on a pitch deck to embossed on a business card
2. **It communicates precision** — a well-designed lettermark signals that the firm pays attention to detail at every level
3. **It builds brand equity fast** — a distinctive geometric mark becomes recognisable quickly in a sector where most identities are interchangeable

The integrated upward arrow transforms the 'E' from a static letterform into a directional mark — communicating exactly what an investment firm communicates to its clients: upward movement.

## Finance brand identity for USA, UK, and Australian markets

EntroEdge operates in the investment and venture sector — a global market where brand credibility crosses borders. The identity was designed to read with equal authority in New York, London, Toronto, and Sydney: the Navy Blue grounding in institutional trust, the Lavender accent signalling modern fintech relevance without sacrificing seriousness.
