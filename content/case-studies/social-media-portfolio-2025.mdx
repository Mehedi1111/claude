## How does Evoke Studio approach social media graphic design?

The approach is brand-first, not trend-first. Most social media designers work from generic templates and trending aesthetics — the result is content that looks current for six months and dated forever after. At Evoke Studio, every post is built from the [brand's established visual identity](/blog/social-media-branding-guide):

1. **Start from brand guidelines** — exact colour codes, typeface weights, spacing rules, and logo clear-space
2. **Design the grid first** — establish the layout proportions before adding any content or copy
3. **Build a master template** — so every subsequent post is a disciplined variation, not a new design built from scratch
4. **Deliver with export presets** — so the client's team can maintain consistency after handoff without a designer

This process is the same whether we are designing 1 post or 50. The system is built once, then applied consistently across every piece of content.

## Why does brand consistency matter in social media design?

A [consistent brand across social media](/blog/website-vs-social-media-do-you-need-both) builds recognition 3–5× faster than inconsistent content. Research from Lucidpress found that consistent brand presentation can increase revenue by up to 33%. For social media specifically:

- Consistent colour use across posts increases brand recognition by up to **80%**
- Posts that match a brand's visual identity receive **38% more engagement** on average than off-brand content
- Instagram accounts with a consistent aesthetic grow their following **23% faster** than accounts without one

This is why [brand identity design](/services/brand-identity) is the foundation of effective social media — not the other way around. You cannot build consistent social media content without a defined visual system to work from. The [brand colours guide](/blog/brand-colors-guide) explains why having the correct hex codes and Pantone values in your brand guidelines matters more than most people realise.

## How do I commission social media design from Evoke Studio?

Social media design commissions at Evoke Studio work best in one of three formats:

**1. One-off post commissions** — individual Instagram or Facebook posts designed to your brand specifications. Turnaround: 24–48 hours. From $25 per post.

**2. Monthly content packages** — 8–20 posts per month, delivered in a single batch or staged across the month. From $15 per post at volume. Includes one revision round per asset.

**3. Template design and handoff** — we design 4–8 master templates to your brand standards in Canva Pro, then hand them over so your team can update copy and images internally without needing a designer each time.

All three options require the same foundation: your brand's visual identity. If you do not have brand guidelines, that is where we begin — see the [logofolio and brand identity work](/portfolio/logofolio-2025) for examples of the brand foundation that makes consistent social media content possible.
