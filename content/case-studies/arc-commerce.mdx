Arc Commerce raised their Series A. They now had the resources to build a real design team, and the visibility that comes with that funding meant their brand inconsistency was becoming a problem.

Their DALL-E generated logo had served as a placeholder for 18 months. It had appeared in pitch decks, on their website, and in partnership agreements. But with different team members using different versions, exported at different sizes, the brand was drifting.

## The Brief

The brief was specific: don't change the logo. It had brand recognition within their market and the founding team felt an emotional connection to it. What they needed was everything else — the system around the mark that would allow it to be used consistently by a team of 40 people across 12 countries.

<Callout type="info">
The most effective brand guidelines are the ones that make it impossible to use the brand incorrectly. They don't just describe the rules — they make the right option the obvious option.
</Callout>

## Phase 1: Logo Vectorisation

Before any brand system work could begin, the logo needed to be properly reconstructed. The DALL-E PNG had been used directly as a file for 18 months, resulting in multiple degraded versions circulating internally.

We reconstructed the mark from scratch, maintaining the proportions and visual character of the original while delivering it as a precision vector with:

- Exact anchor point minimisation (the original auto-traced version had 847 points; our reconstruction had 23)
- Mathematically correct symmetry axes
- Documented anchor point positions for reproduction from specifications

## Phase 3: Typography System

The brand had been using Google Fonts informally — whatever was available in Notion and Google Docs. We established a proper typography hierarchy:

| Level | Typeface | Weight | Use Case |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| Display | Space Grotesk | 700 | Hero headings, feature callouts |
| Heading | Space Grotesk | 600 | Section headers, card titles |
| Body | Inter | 400/500 | UI text, documentation |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | 400 | Code, technical values |

## Phase 4: Brand Guidelines Document

The resulting brand guidelines document runs to 44 pages and covers:

- Logo usage rules with clear space specifications and minimum size requirements
- Misuse examples (showing the most common mistakes)
- Complete colour system with all production values
- Typography rules for digital and print contexts
- Photography and illustration direction
- Component patterns for common marketing materials
