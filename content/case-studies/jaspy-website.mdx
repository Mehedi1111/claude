**Jaspy** is an AI-powered wealth and portfolio management platform built for high-net-worth individuals, family offices, registered investment advisers, and financial institutions across the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia. The platform unifies portfolio data, delivers AI-powered insights, and gives wealth clients a single view of every asset, account, and investment.

The brief: a marketing website that matches the intelligence of the platform — and converts the sophisticated financial buyers Jaspy is built for.

## The design challenge: premium intelligence without FinTech cliché

WealthTech is a category saturated with the same visual language — gradient hero sections, abstract data visualisation icons, and stock photography of business people looking at screens. None of it communicates trust to the buyers who actually manage significant wealth.

Jaspy's buyers — HNW individuals, family offices, and RIAs — are sophisticated. They can read a poorly positioned product in seconds. The design had to communicate:

- **Genuine AI capability** — not surface-level buzzwords, but structured product clarity that signals real technical depth
- **Premium restraint** — wealth management is not a loud category; the design earns trust through precision, not volume
- **Human intelligence** — despite the AI focus, Jaspy is positioned as "human-centric"; the visual language had to hold that tension without contradiction

## Architecture: three product pillars, one coherent story

Jaspy's platform has three core capabilities — Voice Intelligence Hub, Research Engine, and Portfolio Aggregation. A common failure mode for AI platforms is burying this product depth inside vague capability claims.

The site architecture structures these pillars clearly:

1. **Hero positioning** — "Intelligence for Life and Legacy" establishes the premium wealth angle before any product feature is introduced
2. **How It Works** — a three-step process (Connect → Unify → Grow) that makes a complex AI platform immediately legible to non-technical buyers
3. **Platform features** — six core capabilities presented with the specificity that earns trust from financially sophisticated users
4. **Built For** — clear use-case segmentation across investors, family offices, RIAs, entrepreneurs, HNW individuals, and institutions

## Conversion design for high-trust buyers

Wealth management clients do not convert from the first visit. The typical buyer researches for weeks before requesting a demo. The site architecture accounts for this:

- **Multiple entry points to the demo CTA** — placed at friction points where a buyer's confidence is highest, not just above the fold
- **Social proof calibrated to the audience** — "500+ investors & family offices" and "$0.0B+ Assets Aggregated" signal category traction without false precision
- **Feature depth without overwhelm** — enough product specificity to satisfy a diligent buyer without creating cognitive load that stalls decision-making

## Next.js: the right foundation for a wealth platform

For a platform where a buyer's first impression determines whether they trust you with financial data, performance is not a secondary concern. The Jaspy site runs on Next.js with Vercel deployment — delivering:

- Sub-second page loads on every route
- Full SEO control without plugin dependencies
- A codebase that grows with the product without accumulating technical debt
- Static and dynamic rendering strategies matched to each page type
