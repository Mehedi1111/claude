Nexus AI came to us at a pivotal moment. The company had just closed a pre-seed round and was preparing for a Series A raise. Their visual identity — built around a Midjourney-generated geometric mark — had served them well in pitch decks and early presentations.

But as they began preparing investor materials, branded merchandise, and partnerships with enterprise clients, the limitations of their AI-generated logo became impossible to ignore.

## The Challenge

<Callout type="warning" title="The File Situation">
The founding team had one asset: a 1024×1024 PNG exported from Midjourney. No vector file, no colour specifications, no documented brand guidelines. Everything that professional use requires was missing.
</Callout>

The specific technical problems with the Nexus AI logo:

- **Resolution**: At 1024×1024px, the logo degraded visibly at anything above business card size
- **Colour**: The deep blue used in the mark had no CMYK equivalent documented. In test prints, it shifted toward purple
- **Typography**: The wordmark used letterforms that appeared to reference a commercial typeface, but with distorted proportions
- **Paths**: No vector structure existed at all — the mark existed only as a pixel grid

When Nexus AI's printer received the PNG and attempted to reproduce it at trade show dimensions (900mm × 300mm), the output was visibly degraded. The vendor refused to proceed without a vector file.

## The Reconstruction Process

We began with a thorough geometry analysis of the Midjourney output. The octagonal mark had clear mathematical underpinnings — it was based on a regular octagon with internal geometry derived from a 12-unit grid.

The reconstruction proceeded in five phases:

1. **Geometry mapping** — Identified the octagonal base and plotted exact proportions
2. **Path construction** — Built the primary form as 8 anchor points with precisely calculated bezier handles
3. **Internal detail rebuild** — Reconstructed the inner geometric elements maintaining exact proportional relationships
4. **Typography analysis** — Identified the wordmark font as a modified version of an existing typeface, sourced the correct font, and rebuilt the letterforms with accurate kerning
5. **Colour specification** — Established the primary blue as Pantone 2728 C, with verified CMYK and RGB values
