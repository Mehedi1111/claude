Strand Studios had been producing records for independent artists for eight years. In early 2025, founder Elias Strand made a strategic decision to pivot half the studio's capacity toward commercial work — brand sound design, advertising production, and content scoring for agencies.

The pivot required a rebrand. The studio's existing identity was informal, artist-focused. To pitch to agency clients and brand-side marketing teams, Strand needed to look like a professional production partner.

Elias had already found the mark he wanted: an Ideogram-generated retro-industrial letterform — a bold, constructed "S" with subtle vintage industrial detail that felt simultaneously classic and contemporary. He needed everything else built around it in one week.

## Starting Point: One Ideogram Image

The Ideogram export was 1024×1024 pixels. It was a strong visual concept with clear geometric logic — exactly the kind of AI-generated output that rebuilds well into a proper vector system. The challenges were technical: no vector paths, no colour documentation, and no system to extend into stationery, website, or brand usage.

<Callout type="tip" title="When AI Marks Work Well as a Starting Point">
AI-generated marks with clear geometric logic — constructed letterforms, grid-based shapes, clean proportions — translate particularly well to professional vectorisation. The key is not to chase photographic complexity in an AI output, but to identify the geometric structure underneath it and rebuild from that foundation.
</Callout>

## The Brand System Construction

We built the Strand identity across five structured phases over seven days.

**Phase 1 — Vectorisation (Day 1)**
The Ideogram mark was reconstructed as a clean master vector. The constructed letterform was rebuilt on a 24-unit grid, with stroke weights derived from the grid proportions. The industrial detail elements were simplified to their essential forms without losing the character of the original.

**Phase 2 — Colour System (Day 2)**
We established a 2-colour system appropriate to the brand's positioning: Studio Black (`#0D0D0D`) and Analogue Gold (Pantone 8640 C, a warm metallic gold). The restraint of 2 colours signals confidence and positions Strand above more colourful competitors.

**Phase 3 — Typography Hierarchy (Day 3)**
A 3-level type system:
- Headings: Neue Haas Grotesk Display, Black weight — commanding and authoritative
- Body: Neue Haas Grotesk Text, Regular and Medium
- Technical / captions: Courier Prime — a nod to the studio's recording heritage

**Phase 4 — Brand Guidelines (Days 4–5)**
A 28-page brand standards document covering logo usage, colour values across all media, typography specimens, spacing rules, and application examples. Written to be handed to any designer, agency, or production vendor the studio would work with.

**Phase 5 — Stationery (Days 6–7)**
- Business cards: 400gsm Invercote, spot gold foil on Studio Black stock
- Letterhead and compliments slip
- Email signature (HTML)
- Invoice template

## The Outcome

The Strand rebrand launched with the new website on schedule. Within sixty days, the studio had signed three new brand-side clients — a result Elias attributes partly to the quality of the brand materials in their pitch presentations. The business cards, in particular, made an impression.

The brand guidelines document has been distributed to Strand's web developer, their print production partner, their social media agency, and the advertising agencies they work with. The system is being operated consistently across all touchpoints without any additional direction from the studio.
