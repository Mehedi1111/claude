The **September LogoFolio** is a curated collection of logo and brand identity marks completed across client projects in September 2023 — spanning technology startups, professional services firms, consumer brands, and wellness companies.

Each mark in this collection represents a distinct brief and a distinct solution — no templates, no style replication across clients. The collection demonstrates range: the ability to design a precise geometric wordmark for a technology company and a warm typographic mark for a wellness brand using the same rigorous design methodology.

## Design methodology: grid-first, no defaults

Every mark in this collection was built on a mathematical grid system. This is not an aesthetic preference — it is a production requirement. Grid-built logos:

- Reproduce precisely at any scale, from business card to billboard
- Encode correctly in vector format with no ambiguous curves or intersections
- Can be reconstructed by any designer working from the source files
- Hold tolerances for embossing, engraving, and single-colour reproduction

No marks in this collection were generated by AI or adapted from stock sources. Every letterform, every curve, every proportion is a deliberate decision made in response to the specific brief.

## Categories represented

The September collection spans:

- **Technology & SaaS** — geometric marks communicating precision and forward motion
- **Professional services** — typographic wordmarks signalling authority and trust
- **Consumer brands** — marks with warmth and approachability without sacrificing professional finish
- **Wellness & lifestyle** — identity systems balancing premium positioning with human accessibility

## Each mark, a complete system

A logo is not a complete deliverable. Every mark in the September LogoFolio was delivered as a complete brand identity system:

1. **Primary logo** — full colour, white, black, and monochrome variants
2. **Colour system** — primary, secondary, and neutral palettes with Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and HEX specifications
3. **Typography** — primary and secondary typefaces with hierarchy specifications
4. **Usage guidelines** — minimum sizes, clear space rules, approved and prohibited usages
5. **Asset library** — production-ready files for print, digital, and physical applications
