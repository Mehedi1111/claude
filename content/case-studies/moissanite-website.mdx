**Moissanite by Aurelia** is a premium moissanite jewellery brand selling engagement rings, fine jewellery, and luxury accessories to buyers across the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia. When the brief came in, the existing WordPress site was doing the opposite of what a luxury brand needs: slow load times, a generic template aesthetic, and a checkout experience that created friction at exactly the moment buyers needed confidence.

The project was a complete migration from WordPress to Next.js — paired with a ground-up visual redesign built specifically for the premium jewellery market.

## The design challenge: luxury without cliché

Jewellery website design has two failure modes. The first is generic luxury — rose gold backgrounds, script typefaces, soft-focus photography filters that look dated the moment a buyer compares the site to competitors. The second is over-minimalism — stripping out so much visual information that the product loses context and the buyer has no reason to trust the price point.

The Moissanite by Aurelia design navigates between both:

- **Typography precision** — clean, modern type hierarchy that communicates quality without relying on script faces or ornamental decoration
- **Product-first layout** — the photography does the selling; the design frames rather than competes
- **Trust architecture** — the page structure guides buyers from product discovery to purchase confidence with minimal friction

## WordPress to Next.js: the performance case

For a luxury jewellery brand where the average order value is high and the buyer's decision timeline is long, the website performance difference between WordPress and Next.js is directly commercial.

The migration delivered:
- Sub-2s load times replacing 4–6s WordPress average
- Perfect Lighthouse scores for Core Web Vitals
- Full design control without plugin dependencies or theme constraints
- SEO-ready structure from day one — no Yoast patches required

## E-commerce for high-intent luxury buyers

Moissanite jewellery buyers are high-intent: they have researched moissanite vs diamond, they understand the value proposition, and they are ready to purchase — if the website gives them a reason to trust it.

The conversion architecture prioritises:

1. **Immediate quality signalling** — above-the-fold design that communicates premium positioning before any product is shown
2. **Frictionless product discovery** — clean navigation and category structure that matches how luxury jewellery buyers browse
3. **Mobile-first purchase flow** — the majority of luxury jewellery research begins on mobile, even when the purchase completes on desktop
